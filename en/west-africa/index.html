SOLEKO solar, distributed across all of West Africa

West Africa faces a double challenge: an unstable power grid and high energy costs. SOLEKO answers with reliable solar equipment, distributed close to your sites — from Ivory Coast to Nigeria, via Senegal, Ghana, Mali and Burkina Faso. Depending on the market: local stock, short lead times, mastered ECOWAS customs clearance and technical support in French and English.

Whether you equip a home in Abidjan, an SME in Dakar or a rural project in Mali, you get the same certified, warranted equipment, with a dedicated, responsive contact.
