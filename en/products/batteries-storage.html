Lithium LiFePO4 batteries: store your solar energy reliably

Storage is the heart of a reliable solar system. Our lithium LiFePO4 batteries from 5 to 15 kWh guarantee up to 6000 cycles — more than 10 years of daily use — with a built-in BMS that protects against overcharge and deep discharge. Compact, maintenance-free and compatible with most inverters, they are a clear upgrade over lead-acid. Ideal for off-grid sites, frequent outages and backup.
