Solar solutions designed for every use

A panel is not enough — you need the right solution. For homes, we target autonomy and security against outages. For businesses and SMEs, the goal is to cut an often crushing electricity or generator bill. For agriculture, solar pumping and irrigation free farms from diesel. Each solution is sized to your real needs and budget.

Residential

For homes, we target autonomy and security against outages. Kits sized for lighting, essential appliances or the whole house, with lithium storage to get through the night and load-shedding without interruption.

Businesses & SMEs

An electricity or generator bill weighs heavily on an SME. Our solutions cut this cost for good: daytime self-consumption, smart backup, remote supervision. Solar becomes a profitable investment, not an expense.
